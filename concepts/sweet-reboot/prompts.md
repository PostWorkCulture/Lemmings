## Shared art direction prompt

Create a premium game-art terrain concept sheet for a side-view Lemmings-style puzzle adventure. BRAND NEW sweet world, no packaging, no brand logos, no text, no UI. Two completely separate wide landscape scenes stacked vertically, each exactly half of the image; same dark midnight plum background #171523, 24px dark gutter at middle. Each panel is a complete orthographic 2D SIDE-ELEVATION cutaway level, NOT isometric, NOT a perspective vista, NOT a landscape painting with an inaccessible scenic path. Playable terrain takes 55-65% of each scene. Richly sculpted, polished 2.5D hand-painted game-art treatment: gorgeous rounded edge highlights, convincing edible cross-sections, carefully restrained tactile detail, soft ambient occlusion. Crisp silhouette is everything. Large intentional negative spaces inside terrain form traversable tunnels and chambers. Few enormous integrated sweets, never random candy scattered on ordinary platforms. All structures join plausibly and form one purposeful journey. Quiet empty background, no background hills or scenery. Tiny amber entrance hatch on one broad landing, tiny glowing green arched exit on another fully supported landing; these establish scale. NO characters, NO labels, NO watermarks, NO random floating pieces. Controlled palette of three main material colours in each panel. Full artwork extends near panel edges with 5% breathing room. A top-shelf indie game art director's cohesive original confectionery world, elegant and playful rather than sugary visual clutter.

## Sheet 1: Honeycomb Hollow / Ribbonfall Ravine

TOP PANEL: Honeycomb Hollow. One colossal warm golden honeycomb cliff spanning almost full width. Its cutaway shows hexagonal amber candy cells as spacious connected tunnels. A milk-chocolate cap runs along its gently uneven top and dips to reveal two deliberate tunnel entrances. Thick honeycomb columns support the ceiling and floor. A large arch chamber under the centre, tiny syrup pool contained in one carved basin, a brittle thin honeycomb wall clearly separating two chambers. Entrance upper left, exit lower right inside the honeycomb. Route goes down inside the mass, around a central thick column and back up through a ramped chamber. Warm gold, cocoa and cream. BOTTOM PANEL: Ribbonfall Ravine. One colossal satin caramel ribbon folds into an S-shaped set of thick connected switchback ledges; snapped peppermint rods serve as structural braces joining the ribbon's folds. The ribbon is opaque edible toffee, with thick rounded torn cross-sections and gentle stripes, not cloth. Its folded shape defines three traversable shelves and two sheltered chambers. A deliberate broken section near the lower centre needs a short bridge. Start lower left, exit upper right. Amber caramel, deep rose peppermint, ivory. Completely different asymmetric silhouette to top panel.

Luminous semi-translucent teal gummy and dark raspberry gummy, with velvety sugared edges, exquisite soft transmitted light inside the material; dark plum empty air in interior. Start bottom left, route curls clockwise up the internal gummies to exit top right. Pale lime highlights, mostly teal and raspberry. No extra candy props.

## Sheet 4: Bonbon Borough / Peppermint Aqueduct

TOP PANEL: Bonbon Borough. Three giant unwrapped chocolate truffles joined into one broad low landscape. One truffle cut open reveals layered raspberry ganache with a winding hollow tunnel inside. Another forms an arch, with a caramel bridge flowing naturally from broken chocolate shells. Cracked chocolate chunks are large structural buttresses, not scattered decoration. Tiny entrance on left crown, route down through the hollow truffle and across to a right crown exit. Cocoa brown, deep raspberry filling, warm golden caramel, sophisticated soft glossy coating and truffle texture. BOTTOM PANEL: Peppermint Aqueduct. A bold structural landscape built from three enormous intertwined red-and-ivory candy canes laid SIDEWAYS as architectural arches, resting on broad round peppermint rock-candy feet. Broad straight gently curved traversable top and two stepped chambers below. One broken central cane segment creates a narrow gap; hard candy supports and one soft nougat plug communicate different material roles. Start top right, descend through a side arch, return left into lower exit. Ruby red and ivory with tiny turquoise mint accents. No pole forests, no floating separate discs, clearly physically joined sweeping bridge silhouette.

## Sheet 5: Macaron Switchback / Sugar Cathedral

TOP PANEL: Macaron Switchback. Four giant differently sized macarons pressed together into one monumental ascending stepped terrain mass with broken wafer ramps connecting their crisp shells. Elegant pistachio, raspberry and vanilla macarons, realistic ruffled feet and thick cream filling, no pastel rainbow clutter. One macaron cutaway opens into a tunnel through its cream layer; large central overhang creates a shelter; a few deliberate bridging gaps. Entrance lower left, route zigzags through and over the connected macarons to an elevated right exit. Rounded credible confectionery forms, not towers of floating platforms. BOTTOM PANEL: Sugar Cathedral. The chapter finale: one vast rose-gold and pale aqua isomalt sugar geode, shaped into three unequal crystalline cathedral spires with a grand central arch and thick broad nougat foundation. Its broken cross-section reveals a clear 3-stage ascending chamber route inside the sugar, a narrow central neck to mine through, a projecting upper balcony for a final bridge to the glowing green exit. Terrain majority of panel but large clean hollow interior, caramel ligaments structurally join crystals. Entrance bottom right, final exit upper left. Brilliant restrained refracted highlights, frosted sugar faces, no neon clutter, readable walking surfaces, beautiful high-end game art.

## Proposed puzzles

### 1. Honeycomb Hollow

Descend through the honeycomb, cross the syrup basin, then tunnel into the final chamber.

Tools: Miner · Builder · Basher

1. Mine through one thin honeycomb partition to reach the lower shelf.
2. Bridge above the contained syrup basin before releasing the crowd.
3. Bash the last brittle wall; the chocolate roof keeps the route inside the cliff.

### 2. Ribbonfall Ravine

Climb from the lowest fold to the upper ribbon, repairing a broken span on the way.

Tools: Climber · Builder · Platformer

1. Send a climber to the higher fold while holding the group below.
2. Build a connection at the broken lower ribbon.
3. Complete the high horizontal crossing and release everyone along the joined folds.

### 3. Waferworks

Break into the aqueduct, descend through its core and emerge beyond the second arch.

Tools: Digger · Basher · Builder

1. Dig through the cream seam to enter the internal stairwell.
2. Bash through a thick chocolate plug at the central pier.
3. Repair a lower bridge so the crowd can leave the aqueduct together.

### 4. Gummy Grotto

Wind upward through a gummy loop, taking an inner shortcut to the highest shelf.

Tools: Climber · Basher · Platformer

1. Climb to the inner shelf and open a gummy membrane from above.
2. Use a springy gummy pad as the shared lift to the next loop.
3. Extend the high crossing to the liquorice link without blocking the return path.

### 5. Meringue Summit

Descend from airy peaks into a sheltered tunnel and repair the route beneath the central arch.

Tools: Parachuter · Digger · Platformer

1. Give the scout a parachute for the initial high descent.
2. Dig a controlled route through the soft marshmallow core.
3. Bridge the broken wafer landing so the remaining group can take a safe lower path.

### 6. Liquorice Lock

Travel through the lower chambers, unlock the centre and return to the high entrance-side exit.

Tools: Stacker · Climber · Basher · Turner

1. Stack a foothold to reach the central peppermint release.
2. Send a climber around the outer braid to open the upper connection.
3. Bash the fondant passage and turn the crowd back toward the left-hand summit.

### 7. Bonbon Borough

Explore the hollow truffles, then join the low ganache route to the final chocolate crown.

Tools: Miner · Parachuter · Builder

1. Mine through the first ganache chamber instead of breaking its outer shell.
2. Float a scout into the central hollow to prepare the return crossing.
3. Build up to the right truffle and open its final soft ganache connection.

### 8. Peppermint Aqueduct

Start on the right, descend inside the aqueduct and bring the group back along its lower span.

Tools: Attractor · Basher · Builder

1. Hold the crowd while a scout descends inside the right arch.
2. Bash the nougat plug beneath the broken candy-cane roof.
3. Repair the lower span, then release the group toward the left exit.

### 9. Macaron Switchback

Climb through four enormous macarons, alternating between cream tunnels and wafer ridges.

Tools: Miner · Climber · Platformer

1. Mine gently through the pistachio cream to reach the lower vanilla chamber.
2. Climb the inner shell to the high wafer connection.
3. Fill the final horizontal gap without opening a drop through the soft filling below.

### 10. Sugar Cathedral

A long right-to-left ascent through a crystalline interior, ending with a bridge to the high sanctuary.

Tools: Climber · Miner · Builder · Platformer

1. Send a climber up the right tower while the crowd waits in the nougat hall.
2. Mine through the central neck to connect two inner balconies.
3. Build to the final caramel ledge, then secure a flat approach to the high green exit.
