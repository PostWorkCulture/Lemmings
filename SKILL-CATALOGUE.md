# Skill expansion catalogue

The current playable campaign has 16 commands. This is a staged implementation of the requested catalogue, not a claim that every Lemmings 2 ability is implemented. Level trays show only functional abilities supplied by that map. Existing Builder retains its tested 16-step behaviour.

## Playable now

Walker, Blocker, Builder, Digger, Basher, Miner, Platformer, Climber, Parachuter (safe descent), Swimmer, Runner, Jumper, Stacker, Turner (immediate individual reversal), Attractor and Exploder. These are adapted game mechanics; fan steering and tribe-specific animation variants are not implemented yet.

## Remaining catalogue

- Wind and fan control: Ballooner, Hang Glider, Icarus Wings, Jet Pack, Magic Carpet, steerable Parachuter, Surfer, Twister. Add a directional fan tool and explicit air targeting before distributing these across levels.
- Excavation: Club Basher, Fencer, Flame Thrower, Laser Blaster, Scooper, Stomper.
- Construction: Filler, Glue Pourer, Magic Bridge, Planter, Pyramider, Sand Pourer.
- Projectiles: Archer, Bazooka, Mortar, Roper, Spearer, Thrower. Each requires an aiming preview, projectile collision and deterministic replay events.
- Locomotion: Diver, Hopper, Kayaker, Pole Vaulter, Rock Climber, Roller, Shimmier, Skater, Skier, Slider.
- Control: Booter, Magno Booter, SuperLem, and a persistent route-marker version of Turner.

## Implemented level objects

Automatic ladders, safe sliding poles, group trampolines, a moving shuttle with boarding pauses, pressure levers that permanently disable their associated trap, timed crushers and laser gates. Trees, palms, rocks, fossils, mountains, candy scenery and waterfalls provide distinct environments. Waterfalls are background scenery, not solid terrain or a new water-current mechanic.

## Further world mechanics

Drawbridges, wind currents, destructible boulders with distinct materials, rotating gears, water currents, slippery ice and manually aimed projectiles should get dedicated puzzle layouts and full-crowd rescue tests before shipping.
