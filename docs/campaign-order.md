# Thirty-level campaign

The active campaign has three chapters of ten maps:

- Levels 1–10: Freaky Forest — Easy
- Levels 11–20: Mountain Rescue — Medium
- Levels 21–30: Lava Land — Extreme

Only these physical-terrain worlds are offered in the game, practice selector and terrain gallery. Creature Comfort, Sweet Worlds and the five illustrated expansion worlds are archived. Their sources and saved results remain recoverable, but their stars do not count towards active totals or unlocks. Old published gallery links redirect to the current terrain gallery.

Level IDs remain permanent storage keys. `CAMPAIGN` determines display order and `campaignIndex` determines displayed numbering. Never reorder `LEVELS` or renumber its IDs: local and cloud saves use those identities. Completed active maps remain replayable; new unlocks follow the reduced campaign order. A saved current level in an archived world falls back to the opening Forest level, without clearing results or player identities.

Visible terrain in the active worlds is rendered from the same material mask used by walking, climbing and excavation. Illustrated route-over-art maps must not be added back without a separate redesign and approval.

All 30 active levels release 10 lemmings. Their existing rescue targets, useful tool allowances and target times are retained, together with existing player results.

27 maps fit in the playable viewport. Only Moonlit Canopy (10), The Hidden Summit (19) and Up the Chimney (29) use vertical scrolling. No gameplay viewport supports horizontal scrolling.
