# Pick ’n’ Mix material pass

Built-in image generation was used for the nine-material atlas at `assets/terrain/sweet-materials.png`. The final atlas was refined once to reduce crumpling and keep the terrain readable. Swirls, wrapper seams, centred type, candy-watch embossing and bead shading are drawn by the game renderer.

The art renderer preserves the shipped collision masks, exit positions, stock and rescue routes. It does not regenerate terrain from the new texture. No other chapter artwork is changed.

## Initial generation prompt

Create a production-quality 2D game MATERIAL TEXTURE ATLAS for a charming British retro sweet-shop platform puzzle game. Square image, precisely 3 columns and 3 rows of equally sized square tiles, zero gutters, zero borders. Each tile fills its entire square edge to edge, no transparent areas, no silhouettes, no objects, no text, no lettering or logos. Painterly high-end mobile adventure game finish, richly tactile surfaces, restrained highlights, fine physical detail, soft rounded lighting. These are close-up MATERIALS to map onto giant sweet terrain. Tile row1 col1: turquoise blue metallic sweet wrapper with golden yellow narrow horizontal stripes near top and bottom, dense subtle crinkles in foil, central cyan area unprinted. Row1 col2: milky cream fudge in upper half smoothly marbled into raspberry pink chew lower half, tiny sugar grain, soft pulled toffee striations. Row1 col3: lavender cellophane over pale violet chalk tablets, delicate vertical tablet ribs, silken wrinkles and lavender highlights. Row2 col1: pearly cream waxed sweet-roll paper with faint embossed pink hearts scattered widely, softly crinkled and translucent. Row2 col2: intense royal blue metallic foil with small angular magenta and yellow flashes mostly toward edges, shiny but not overexposed. Row2 col3: sunset orange and deep cherry red pouch foil split roughly vertically, fine crinkles and soft highlights. Row3 col1: pink and butter yellow diagonal striped glossy chew wrapper, subtle folds. Row3 col2: red rhubarb and golden custard hard-boiled candy material, smooth glassy interleaved marbling with finely frosted sugar specks. Row3 col3: close-up softly granular white sugar candy chalk, pearly white and neutral grey shading, porous tiny crystalline grains, no colored patches. Keep all tiles fully opaque, uniform lighting from top left, no background, no cast shadows, no dark outlines. This is a usable texture sheet not a presentation board.

Reduce the number and sharpness of creases by 85 percent. Large smooth satin surfaces with broad gently curved highlights, a handful of soft paper folds near edges. NO sharp triangular foil facets, NO rocky-looking crumples.
